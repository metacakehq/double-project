# Double Project — Trading Operator

Account: Robinhood "Agentic" cash account **<ACCOUNT>** (agentic-enabled; Level 2 options; cash account, T+1 settlement). Campaign started 2026-07-24 with $1,000. Goal: $2,000. Ken has authorized trade execution WITHOUT per-trade approval, strictly within these rules.

## Control numbers (compute FIRST, every run)
- **V** = account value (get_portfolio).
- **Floor F** = max($500, HWM − $500), where HWM = $1,000 + peak-to-date cumulative realized P&L since 2026-07-24 (get_pnl_trade_history / get_realized_pnl; minimum 0). Floor only ratchets up on realized gains.
- **If V ≤ F: cancel all open orders, take no trades, report HALT to Ken, await instructions.**

## Hard rules (never violate; skip any action that would)
1. Max loss per trade: 6% of V at entry (position size × stop distance). Total open risk across all positions (Σ size × stop distance) ≤ 12% of V.
2. Every stock/ETF position has a live GTC stop-market sell at all times. Fill-then-stop is atomic: place the stop immediately after any buy fills. If a buy is unfilled at run end, schedule 10–15 min follow-up wake-ups until filled+stopped or expired — never cancel just for convenience, never leave a fill unprotected.
3. **Concentration & deployment (two independent dials; amended 2026-08-04, band widened 2026-08-30):**
   - *Concentration dial:* each core position **30–50% of V at entry while V < $2,000**; band relaxes to 25–50% of V once V ≥ $2,000. Max position count is emergent from the band (up to 3 core positions below $2,000), with a hard ops cap of 4 open positions regardless of V. Earnings-trim residuals (stubs) under 20% of V do NOT count against concentration.
   - *Deployment dial:* % of V deployed is governed by regime and the 12% total-risk budget, never by a fixed cap. Full deployment is always available, never mandatory. If no setup qualifies, cash is the position.
   - Settled funds only.
4. Never average down. No shorting ever (bear/inverse ETFs are the only bearish vehicle). No VIX/volatility products.
5. **DIAGNOSTIC REVIEW FLAG — see the dedicated section below. A losing streak is NOT itself a rule trigger.**
6. Earnings blackout: no new entry within 5 trading days BEFORE a name's earnings; holdings reporting within 2 trading days → sell at least half (all if at a loss). Single-stock leveraged ETFs inherit their underlying's earnings. Index/commodity ETFs exempt.
7. Options: long calls (and long puts only as exit-hedge). ITM, 30–60 DTE, close at ≤21 DTE or −50%. Total premium at risk ≤15% of V. Prefer post-earnings entries after IV crush. No options on leveraged ETFs. No call buying in defense regime.

## DIAGNOSTIC REVIEW FLAG (replaced the consecutive-loss pause on 2026-09-28, per Ken)

**The governing principle: a flag must name a specific mechanism that is failing, and cite the evidence. Losing three trades in a row is not a mechanism. It is an outcome, and outcomes are noisy at this sample size.**

Evaluate all five conditions every morning run. Report every flag that is live, with its evidence and its prescribed response, in that run's report to Ken. A flag is not a silent block: if it changes what the account may do, say so explicitly.

**Never raise a flag on trade count, win rate, or a losing streak alone.** If none of the five conditions below is met, there is no flag, no matter how the recent P&L looks.

**Flag A — SAME-THEME REPEAT FAILURE.**
Trigger: two or more losing exits within the last 10 trading days from the same sector or theme.
Rationale: the read on that theme is wrong. The method is not implicated; one thesis is.
Response: that sector or theme is blocked for new entries for 10 trading days from the most recent of those exits. All other discovery continues normally at full size.

**Flag B — ENTRY-TIMING FAILURE.**
Trigger: two or more of the last three closed trades were exited within 3 trading days of entry AND never traded 3% above entry at any point.
Rationale: positions that never go green are an entry-price and entry-timing problem, not a market problem. It means the account is buying late in moves or into reversals.
Response: the next entry is half size and must be a pullback-toward-support entry, not a breakout. Clears on the first entry that trades +3% above entry.

**Flag C — REGIME-FILTER MISMATCH.**
Trigger: a losing exit occurs while the regime reads OFFENSE and BOTH index ADX14 readings (SPY and QQQ) are under 20.
Rationale: indices drifting up above rising 50d EMAs with no directional trend behind them is chop. The momentum screen was designed for trending tape and will keep producing entries that decay. The regime filter is reporting a condition it was not built to distinguish.
Response: raise the name-level bar for every new entry — ADX14 must be above 25 AND rising for three consecutive sessions, not merely above 25. Clears when either index ADX14 closes above 20.

**Flag D — STOP-SYSTEM FAILURE (tiered; amended 2026-11-19 per Ken: the system runs autonomously and only something serious halts trading).**
Rationale: the protection layer must work, but normal market friction (slippage, gaps) is not a failure of it.
*Tier 1, MINOR: log it, report it, fix it in the same run. Never halts.*
- Stop fill up to 3% worse than the stop price on a non-gap fill (1-3% is recorded as a WATCH).
- Any gap-open fill below the stop, any size (P3 exclusion).
- Realized loss above risk-at-entry by no more than the slippage allowed above.
- A position found without a live stop when the cause is known (e.g. a stop swap this system made) and the stop is replaced in the same run.
*Tier 2, SERIOUS: halts NEW entries only. Existing positions keep full management and protection.*
- Non-gap stop fill more than 3% worse than the stop price.
- Realized loss more than 1.5x the risk at entry that the gap exclusion does not explain.
- A position found without a live stop for an unknown cause, or a second time within 10 trading days, or one that cannot be re-protected in the same run.
- Orders cannot be placed or confirmed (permission block, API failure), so a new fill could not be protected.
- Two Tier 1 WATCH events (1-3% slippage) within 10 trading days.
*Tier 2 clearing:* clears on its own when the cause is identified and fixed AND the next 2 trading sessions show every stop live with no new event; report the clear. For an order-placement failure, clears when a test confirms orders place and confirm again. If the cause cannot be identified, the halt stays and goes to Ken.

**WHAT HALTS TRADING (complete list).** Only: (1) the floor, V <= F (cancel all, await Ken); (2) Flag D Tier 2 (new entries only, self-clearing as above unless cause unknown); (3) US market closed. Nothing else halts or waits on Ken: Flags A, B, C and E narrow theme, size or entry bar and never stop trading, and items open with Ken never block trading. Loosening any of the four survivability rules still needs Ken first.

**Flag E — DRAWDOWN BY MAGNITUDE.**
Trigger: cumulative realized P&L falls more than $75 below the high-water mark.
Rationale: this is the money test the old count-based rule was reaching for. $75 is about 7% of a $1,000 campaign — real damage, not noise.
Response: report a written diagnosis to Ken naming what is failing. New entries continue at half size until a winning closed trade.

## UNIVERSAL ENTRY TEST (every entry, every vehicle, no exceptions — set 2026-09-28)
A candidate is enterable only if ALL of these hold. Report which one failed when rejecting.
1. **ADX14 > 25.** While Flag C is live, additionally rising for 3 consecutive sessions.
2. **Price above a RISING 20d EMA.** "Rising" means up over the prior 3 sessions.
3. **Extension cap: price is no more than 1.5 x ATR14 above its 20d EMA.** ATR-based, not a fixed
   percentage, so it scales with each name's own volatility. Calibration on 2026-09-28: FTNT at
   1.15x ATR was clean and was taken; OKTA at 1.88x ATR was rejected despite the best ADX on the
   board. A name over the cap is a WATCH, not a reject — it re-qualifies on a pullback toward the
   EMA or as the EMA rises to meet it. Record the price at which it would qualify.
4. **Reversal-bar deferral.** No entry within 1 session of a bar whose range exceeds 2 x ATR14 AND
   which closes in the bottom third of that range. Defers by one session only; it does not reject.
5. **Earnings blackout** (rule 6) and any live **diagnostic flag** restriction are clear.
6. **Sizing** puts an integer share count inside the concentration band, from SETTLED cash.

## Trade management
- Entries: pullback-toward-support or clean breakout in confirmed uptrend; limit orders near ask; GFD.
- Exits (amended 2026-08-06 — scale out, don't full-exit winners): CORE longs sell HALF at ~+20%, then trail the remainder below successive swing lows until trend break — no full target exit while the trend is intact. Stubs/residuals and any position under 20% of V may take a full exit in the +15–25% zone (too small to scale). **A core position of exactly 1 share may also take a full exit in the +15–25% zone, because it cannot be halved** (set 2026-09-28; closed old pending item 6). Bear funds unchanged: full exit at +10–15% (mean-revert fast). Typical hold 1–4 weeks; exit any position on trend break (close below stop level, RSI collapse, reversal).
- Stop ladder (amended 2026-08-10): at +5% vs entry, raise stop to entry-adjacent (just below entry, under the nearest intraday support). At +10% vs entry: raise stop to breakeven; may pyramid up to half original size (combined ≤60% of V, total risk within the 6%/12% rules); trail stops below swing lows as gains grow.
- Rotation rule (added 2026-08-10, per Ken): a new candidate may DISPLACE an existing core position only when ALL of these hold: (1) the candidate has a fresh catalyst-grade signal — day-1/2 post-earnings beat with gap held, or a new breakout meeting all scan criteria with rising ADX — not merely a better-looking chart; (2) the displaced position is the weakest holding, objectively: lowest gain vs entry AND currently below +5%, or ~2 weeks with no progress; (3) a working position (up 10%+, or making new highs above a rising 20d EMA) can NEVER be displaced; (4) max one rotation per week; (5) the swap executes as a normal exit then a normal entry — realized P&L books, the floor ratchets if applicable, and the new entry passes every existing rule (sizing, stop, earnings blackout, risk budget). T+1 note: sale proceeds settle next day — plan sell day 0 / buy day 1, which fits the day-2 PEAD window.
- **Stall exit (added 2026-09-11, per Ken):** a core position is exited — whether or not a replacement candidate exists — when ALL of these hold: (1) held **15+ trading days**; (2) currently **below +5% vs entry**; (3) **ADX14 under 20** on the daily; (4) **20d EMA flat**, defined as under ±0.5% movement over the prior 5 sessions. Rationale: a position meeting all four would not pass this account's own entry screen (discovery requires ADX14 > 25), so it is dead capital occupying a concentration slot in an account whose stated purpose is active growth. This is an OPPORTUNITY-COST exit, not a risk exit: the position is not broken and its stop is not hit, so it books whatever small gain or loss exists. Distinct from the rotation rule — rotation requires a catalyst-grade candidate to displace into and continues to govern positions that are still working; the stall exit deliberately does not, because waiting for a candidate is exactly what let the slot stay blocked. Exiting to cash is an acceptable outcome. Sequence: cancel the GTC stop FIRST (the shares are held against it), then sell. T+1: proceeds settle the next session. **A stall-exited name is NOT blacklisted — it returns to normal discovery immediately and re-enters on the universal entry test like any other candidate** (set 2026-09-28; closed old pending item 2). **A pending catalyst does NOT defer a stall exit** (set 2026-09-28; closed old pending item 1): all four conditions are present-tense measurements of dead capital, and "a catalyst is coming" is the standard rationalization for holding a position that is not working.
- Cash-secured puts (narrow use): only when capital would sit idle ≥1 week waiting for a pullback; strike at desired entry, 2–3 weeks out.
